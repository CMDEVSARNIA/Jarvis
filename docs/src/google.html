H.U.X.L.E.Y.Google setup · part 1 of 2

> Connect Google

Lets Huxley read your calendar and add or change events directly, read your Gmail and save reply drafts, and search your Google Docs. About 15 minutes, once, on the laptop. Free: no card needed.

Part 1 · Your own Google "app" for Huxley

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the Google account Huxley should use. Accept the terms if asked. Ignore any "free trial" or billing offers: none of this needs billing.
  2. Make a project: go straight to console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate → name it Huxley → Create. Or use the project picker (top left, next to the Google Cloud logo; it says "Select a project") → New project. Then check that box at the top left says "Huxley". An existing "My First Project" works too.
  3. Switch on three services: ☰ menu → APIs & Services → Library. Search for each, open it, press Enable: Google Calendar API · Gmail API · Google Drive API
  4. Set up the sign-in screen: ☰ → Google Auth Platform (older name: OAuth consent screen) → Get started. App name Huxley · support email: yours → Next · Audience: External → Next · contact email: yours → Next · tick agree → Create.
  5. Stop the sign-in expiring every week: Google Auth Platform → Audience → Publish app → Confirm. Status becomes "In production". You don't need Google's verification for your own use; you'll just see a warning when signing in (step 8).
  6. Make Huxley's key: Google Auth Platform → Clients → Create client → Application type Desktop app → name Huxley → Create. A box shows the Client ID and Client secret. Copy both into Notepad now (or press Download JSON): Google may not show the secret again.
Keep the Client secret private, like a password. Don't paste it into chats or emails; you'll paste it only into Huxley's own window in step 7.

> Sign Huxley in

Part 2 · Connect (2 minutes)

  1. In the Huxley folder, double-click connect-google.bat. Paste the Client ID (Ctrl+V, Enter), then the Client secret (Ctrl+V, Enter: nothing shows while pasting, that's normal).
  2. Your browser opens Google's sign-in. Pick your account. On "Google hasn't verified this app" click Advanced → Go to Huxley (unsafe). That warning is about your own app, which only you use.
  3. Tick every box (calendar, Gmail, Drive) → Continue. The page says "Huxley is connected to Google". The black window lists what Huxley can now do. An unticked line means you missed a box: run connect-google.bat again.
Done when the GOOGLE light in the top bar of Huxley's screen turns on (within a few seconds, no restart needed). Ask: What's on my calendar tomorrow?

Things to try

Calendar

Add dentist Friday at 3pm

Move my dentist appointment to 4

Cancel tomorrow's lunch

Huxley adds events straight away, but asks you to say yes before moving or deleting one.

Email

Anything important in my email today?

Read me the one from the bank

Draft a reply saying Friday works

Drafts wait in Gmail → Drafts. Huxley has no way to send email: you send it.

Documents

Find my notes about the trip

What did I write about the budget?

Searches Google Docs, Sheets and Slides. Read-only.

Safety built in

Huxley treats text in emails and documents as information, never instructions, so a sneaky email can't make it do things.

To disconnect: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Huxley → Remove access.

> Traffic & voice

Border waits: nothing to set up

On the next start Huxley asks which crossing you use (press Enter for Blue Water Bridge). Live waits in both directions appear as a BRIDGE line in the ATMOS panel and in your morning briefing. Ask: How's the bridge?

Drive times with traffic: free TomTom key (optional, 5 minutes)

  1. Go to developer.tomtom.com → Register (free, no card) → confirm your email.
  2. In the Dashboard, open Keys and copy your key (one is made for you).
  3. Huxley asks for it once on its next start ("Free TomTom key…"): Ctrl+V, Enter. Also asked: your usual commute destination. Missed it? Run .venv\Scripts\python -m jarvis.configure in the Huxley folder; it's under "Travel".

Ask: How long to drive to Port Huron? · How's my commute?

Huxley's voice on your phone

Tap VOX at the bottom of Huxley's screen and pick a voice; Huxley says a test line. Your choice is remembered on that device. If every choice sounds the same (Samsung phones often do this), set the phone's own voice:

Settings → search "Text-to-speech" → Preferred engine: Speech Services by Google → ⚙ → Install voice data → English (United Kingdom) → try the voices and pick a male one. Then reopen Huxley.

If something goes wrong

"Access blocked: Huxley has not completed the Google verification process"Step 5 wasn't done (the app is still in Testing). Publish it, then run connect-google.bat again.
"Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch"The client isn't a Desktop app. Make a new client (step 6) as Desktop app.
"That Google API isn't switched on"Enable it in step 3, wait a minute, ask again.
"Google sign-in has expired"Run connect-google.bat again (press Enter to keep the saved Client ID).
"wasn't given Gmail/Docs access"A box wasn't ticked. Run connect-google.bat and tick every box.
