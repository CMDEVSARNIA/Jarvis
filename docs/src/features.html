FEATURE GUIDE · OVERVIEW01
H.U.X.L.E.Y.01 · TALKING
01

Talking to Huxley

Huxley starts speaking as soon as its first sentence is ready, and keeps listening for your follow-up after it answers, so it feels like a conversation, not a walkie-talkie.

WAY TO TALKHOW IT WORKS
Ctrl+Alt+SpaceTalk from any app: Huxley comes to the front and listens.
Hold SpaceListens the whole time you hold it, even through long pauses. Sends when you let go. Best for long questions.
Click MICListens until you've been quiet for a moment, then sends. Click again to send immediately.
WAKE WORD onAlways listening: start with “Huxley…”. Background talk without “Huxley” is ignored.
CONVERSATION onAfter a spoken answer, ask a follow-up without pressing anything. Say “that's all”, “thanks Huxley” or stay quiet for 7 seconds to end it.
TypeType in the box and press Enter. Replies still appear in the transcript, and are spoken if VOICE is on.
TelegramMessage your bot from anywhere. Same Huxley, same memory. Send /reset to start a fresh conversation.

Getting the timing right

WAIT setting

How long a pause means you've finished: 1.5, 2.5, 4 or 6 seconds. Huxley also adapts: it answers sooner when your sentence sounds complete, and waits longer when you trail off (“and…”, “so…”, “um…”).

Best voice

Use the Edge window Huxley opens. Edge has natural voices such as “Microsoft Ryan”. While Huxley speaks it stops listening, so it doesn't hear itself.

Think harder

For a tough question (comparisons, planning, decisions), say “think hard about…”, “take your time” or “step by step”, or press THINK HARDER (it turns orange) before asking. It applies to that one question, gives a slower, more careful answer, and costs a few times more than a normal question.

INSTANT ACKNOWLEDGMENTSWhen Huxley needs a moment (weather, a web search, your calendar), it says so right away: “Checking the weather.” Silence followed by an answer is normal only for very quick replies.

Your day

FEATUREWHAT TO SAY / WHAT HAPPENS
Morning briefingAt 8:00 (or when you first open Huxley that day): weather and what to wear, today's calendar, reminders and to-dos. Spoken, and sent to Telegram. Press BRIEFING any time.
Evening recapAt 9 PM: what's coming tomorrow and anything still open.
Reminders“Remind me tomorrow at 9 to call the landlord.” “Remind me every weekday at 8 to take vitamins.” Spoken on the PC and sent to your phone. Missed while Huxley was off? It tells you when it starts, marked late.
Timers“Set a 10-minute timer for the pasta.”
Manage reminders“What reminders do I have?” “Cancel the landlord one.”
Lists“Add milk to my shopping list.” “Read my to-do list.” “Remove number 2.” “Clear the shopping list.”
Weather & time“What's the weather?” “Will it rain in Ottawa tomorrow?” “What time is it in Tokyo?”
NudgesAutomatic, 7 AM–10 PM only: a heads-up 10 minutes before calendar events, and one warning a day when rain or snow is likely in the next few hours.

Calendar

“What's on today?” “Am I free Friday afternoon?” “Add lunch with Maria Friday at 12:30.” Adding opens a pre-filled event in your browser; check it and click Save. Huxley can read your calendar but never changes it on its own.

Connect Google Calendar

  • calendar.google.com → ⚙ Settings
  • Under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar
  • Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format

Connect Outlook.com

  • ⚙ Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars
  • Publish a calendar → Can view all details
  • Copy the ICS link
THENIn the Huxley folder run .venv\Scripts\python -m jarvis.configure, press Enter through the answers and paste the link at Calendar iCal link (Ctrl+V works). Treat that link like a password: never paste it into a chat.

On your PC

FEATUREWHAT TO SAY / WHAT HAPPENS
Your screen“What's on my screen?” “Help me with this error.” Huxley hides itself for a second, takes a screenshot, and comes back.
ClipboardCopy some text, then: “Summarize what I copied.” “Fix the grammar in what I copied and put it back.” Then paste with Ctrl+V.
Page viewer“Show me the CBC news page.” “Pull up that YouTube video.” “Show me a map of the CN Tower.” Opens inside Huxley. Use the address box, READER (clean text), BROWSER or CLOSE. Login sites like Gmail open in your normal browser.
Music & volume“Pause the music.” “Next song.” “Mute.” “Volume to 30.” Works with Spotify, YouTube and anything else playing.
Lock, sleep, power“Lock the computer.” “Sleep in 30 minutes.” “Shut down.” Huxley asks first, and a shutdown always gives you at least a minute: say “cancel”.
Folders & files“Open my Downloads.” “Find my tax return and open it.” Searches Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music and Videos.
Apps & websites“Open Spotify.” (apps listed in config\apps.json) “Open YouTube in a tab.”

Protocols: your own routines

Describe a routine once: “Create a work mode protocol: say ‘Let's get to work’, open Gmail, play my music, set volume to 30 and read my to-do list.” Huxley reads the steps back and saves them when you agree. After that, just say “Work mode.” Also: “What protocols do I have?”, “Delete movie night.”

A protocol can open websites and apps, control music and volume, read a list, check the weather, wait, and say something. Nothing more, so a routine can never do anything risky.

SAFETY BUILT INHuxley never opens programs or scripts (.exe, .bat, …) it finds, only documents, pictures and media in your own folders. The viewer only fetches public websites, never devices on your home network.

Memory & privacy

Huxley remembers in three ways, and you can see and clear every one of them.

WHATHOW IT WORKS
Facts about youWhen you mention something lasting (“My sister's name is Maria”, an allergy, a routine), Huxley saves it and says so. “What do you know about me?” “Forget the allergy one.” Or press MEMORY to see the list and click FORGET. It never saves passwords or card numbers.
Recent conversationsAfter a restart, Huxley recalls your last dozen exchanges. RESET (or /reset on Telegram) clears them.
Your stuffReminders, lists, protocols and settings stay on your laptop, in the Huxley folder.

Where everything lives

FILE (IN THE JARVIS FOLDER)CONTAINS
.envYour settings and keys. Change them with the settings tool, never by sharing the file.
data\memory.jsonFacts about you
data\transcript.jsonlText of recent conversations (delete to wipe)
data\reminders.json, lists.json, protocols.jsonYour reminders, lists and routines

Privacy rules Huxley follows

  • Looks at your screen or clipboard only when you ask in that message
  • Only you can use your Telegram bot
  • The Huxley screen only works on your laptop
  • Your words, screenshots and copied text go to Claude to be answered; nothing else is shared
  • Speech-to-text runs through your browser's (Microsoft's or Google's) speech service

Changing settings

In the Huxley folder: click the address bar, type cmd, press Enter, then run .venv\Scripts\python -m jarvis.configure. Press Enter to keep an answer, type - to clear one. It covers your key, model, name, city, units, briefing and recap times, Telegram and calendar.

Extra options in .env: TALK_HOTKEY=ctrl+shift+j · NUDGES=false · HUD_FULLSCREEN=true · AUTO_UPDATE=false

Updates & help

SITUATIONDO THIS
Getting new featuresAutomatic each start, or tray icon → Check for updates. It asks only about new settings.
Something isn't workingDouble-click checkup.bat (safe to photograph and share), and tray icon → Open log.
